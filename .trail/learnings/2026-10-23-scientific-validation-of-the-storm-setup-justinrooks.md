## Tried and ruled out

Anvil's installed pinned SHARPpy 1.4.0a5 gives STP-CIN 0.666667 at CIN=-100 with other terms one; current Anvil gives 0.4 and jumps at -50. All 47 focused Anvil tests pass, so they are not an independent scientific oracle. A suspected SHIP discrepancy disappears when both paths use the same MU parcel. The effective-shear EL variable name does not itself imply an AGL/MSL calculation error. Do not recreate those false findings. No outcome-labelled historical dataset was established.

## Watch out

Signal's aggregate exact classification matches valid time, not necessarily the initial surface snapshot's cycle; each Anvil profile does have its own exact-cycle surface. Canonical ingredients replace corresponding native values; they are not independent forecasts. Surface CIN/LCL can currently masquerade as mixed-layer fields. Low CAPE's hard weak gate and generic High confidence warrant bounded validation, not new unvalidated thresholds. The deep fixture's two partial-CAPE integration paths disagree; this is an unresolved numerical-definition concern, not an established defect. Never turn realization=realized into observed storms, or missing CIG into no hazard.
